A CLEAR, LOCAL WORKFLOW

Signals, not assumptions.

01

Find facial landmarks

MediaPipe estimates eye and mouth landmark positions in your browser. No identity or demographic traits are inferred.

02

Watch changes over time

Eye closure uses elapsed time, with a configurable early warning and a 30-second alarm. A five-minute test ends early only after a sustained warning is confirmed for 5–10 seconds; normal blink changes alone do not stop it. A short alert-state calibration adjusts the EAR threshold.

03

Keep a local record

Five-minute reports show confirmed drowsiness, possible drowsiness signals, appears awake, or undetermined. Blink-only warnings do not stop the camera; sustained concerns must persist 5–10 seconds. Session reports export as JSON or CSV. No video or images are saved.

PRIVACY & SAFETY

Designed to stay on your device.

The camera is requested only after you switch monitoring on. Frames are analyzed in this browser; they are not uploaded or stored. The landmark model and browser library load from Google’s CDN.

Reports and contact details use this browser’s local storage, which is not encrypted. Use a trusted device. Delete reports or contacts at any time. This demonstration does not provide emergency response, and no automated call or SMS provider is configured.

Stop driving if you feel sleepy. Pull over somewhere safe and rest. Detection may fail with poor lighting, occlusion, glasses, camera placement or missing landmarks. “Appears awake” means no drowsiness signal was recorded; it is not a safety guarantee. Alcohol/drug impairment is not assessed.
